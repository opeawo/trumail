FAQs

Find out all you need to know.

How little can I start with?+

Some investment opportunities will start from ₦1,000. Minimums vary by product and are shown before you commit.

Who holds my money?+

Where a licence is required, your funds are held and managed by licensed banks, asset managers and trustees. OurMonee runs the app, the community and sourcing of opportunities.

How does fractional property ownership work?+

Many members contribute toward one property or plot. Each member's share matches what they put in, and the asset is held directly or through a structured vehicle on members' behalf.

Do you give cash loans to businesses?+

Our focus is financing productive assets such as equipment, machinery, vehicles and inventory that help a business earn more.

How do I join the weekly business pitch?+

The ₦1 million weekly programme is proposed. Join the early access list and we'll tell members first when entries open.

How does OurMonee make money?+

From distributing investment products, property transactions, financing, management fees, partnerships and transaction services.
